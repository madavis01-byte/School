Who we are

Education should make more room for the future.

Traditional school was built over a hundred years ago to train people for desk jobs and assembly lines. It teaches you to memorize facts, pass tests, and race against artificial deadlines.

Sandbox Academy is different.

Founded in 2026 by a 14-year-old creator who realized the education system was broken, Sandbox Academy is a decentralized, open-world learning platform designed for the future. We don’t care about standardized test scores or GPA. We care about your dreams.

Whether you want to build reality-bending ARGs, launch a viral YouTube channel, act in films, or code indie video games, we give you the tools to start right now—no matter your age.

🧩 Our 3 Golden Rules

The things we will always protect.

01

Your Pace, Your Power

There are absolutely no deadlines here. True creativity takes time. You finish your project when you are proud of it, not when a clock tells you to.

02

No Shame, Only Adjustments

Grades in the traditional sense don't exist here. If you are struggling with a concept like animation or coding, we don't give you a failing grade or extra homework. We simply tap the brakes, figure out what you need, and pivot to something you enjoy.

03

Zero Restrictions

You start exactly at your skill level. A quick placement minitest unlocks your personalized skill tree. If a 2nd grader is ready to code 3D video games, we let them code. No gatekeeping.

Why “Sandbox”?

You get the world. You build what comes next.

In video games, a “sandbox” mode means you have unlimited resources, total freedom, and no set path. You are dropped into a world and told to create.

That is exactly what we do for education. We give you the sandbox—you build the world.

There is no wrong path, only the next interesting thing.
